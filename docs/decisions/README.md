## Context common to all of them

The author's goals, in priority order:

1. **Brand and exposure in the NVIDIA ecosystem** — the actual objective
2. **Open courseware** — a means to (1), not an independent goal
3. **Direct revenue from a book** — real but secondary

This ranking is the premise of every decision here. The operative rule it
produces:

> **Never let a monetization decision reduce reach.**

Rough arithmetic behind the ranking: the DGX Spark shipped in late 2025 to an
installed base plausibly in the tens of thousands. A niche technical book
converts a low single-digit percentage of its addressable audience. At roughly
$30 with a 70% royalty, a good outcome is four figures and an excellent one is
low five. Ecosystem exposure — DevRel roles, consulting, conference speaking —
is worth an order of magnitude more. When the two conflict, reach wins.
